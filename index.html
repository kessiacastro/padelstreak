On the court

One tap between points

Tap the bottom for your pair, the top for theirs. The zones are big enough to hit while you're catching your breath and walking back to position.

  • Every padel rule. Advantage, golden point, or the new star point.
  • Tie-breaks and super tie-breaks, with the real serve rotation.
  • Serve tracking — who serves, from which side, and when it changes.
  • Undo, because everyone taps the wrong side eventually.
Nothing else tracks this

Who's actually winning your points?

Turn it on and winning a point asks one quick question: was that you, your partner, or their mistake? It's the difference between knowing you won and knowing why.

  • Your finishing share of your team's points, and how it trends.
  • Split by partner — who you actually click with.
  • On the big points, who steps up when the game is on the line.
  • Entirely optional. Leave it off and scoring stays one tap.
After the match

Stats that tell you where to work

Not a skill grade somebody invented. Serving and returning, the points that decide games, and which side of the court you're strong on — all derived from the points you tapped.

  • Serve and return — games held, break points saved and taken.
  • Under pressure — break, golden, star, set and match points.
  • Deuce side vs advantage side. Worth knowing before you pick sides.
  • Every rate shows its counts, so you know what to trust.
Not every session is a match

Just training? Skip the score

Start a practice session and the scoreboard disappears. You get a clock and your effort — heart rate, calories and distance — with nothing to tap between drills.

  • No score, no games, no sets. There's nothing to keep track of.
  • Still saved to Apple Health as a workout, same as a match.
  • Kept out of your stats. Practice never touches your win rate or season points.
  • It still shows in your history, so the week's court time is honest.
Your regulars

Who you win with, who you struggle against

Every match remembers who you played with and where. Over a season that turns into the thing padel groups argue about most — settled.

  • Partners & Rivals with win rates and finishing splits.
  • Season points — up to 100 a match, adding up across the year.
  • Heart rate, calories and distance from your Watch, saved to Apple Health.

Earn points even when you lose

Every match is worth up to 100 points, and nothing can ever take them away. Win and you bank a lot. Play well and lose and you still bank plenty — which is the whole idea.
