Made by players, for players

Padel Streak exists because we wanted a scoreboard that actually works mid-match — big zones you can hit without looking, while you're catching your breath between points.

And because the stats afterwards should come from what really happened on court, not from a rating somebody invented. Every number here traces back to a point you played.

Every feature came from something that annoyed us during a game. Found something missing? Tell us — that's how the last few got made.

Built for the court

Full padel rules on your Apple Watch: advantage, golden point, or the new star point. Tie-breaks, an optional super tie-break for the deciding set, and serve tracking that follows the real rotation.

Know who's actually winning the points

Optionally tag who finished each point — you, your partner, or an opponent's error. It's one extra tap, and it's the thing nothing else tracks: your share of your team's points, your finishing trend, and how it splits with each partner.
