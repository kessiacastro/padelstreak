Padel Streak

Privacy Policy

Effective 5 September 2026

Padel Streak ("the app") is built to keep your data yours. This policy explains what the app uses, how it's stored, and what is (and isn't) shared. Plain and short, because that's the whole point.

The short version

  • We do not collect, receive, sell, or share your data.
  • Everything the app records stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and syncs only through your own private iCloud account, which the developer cannot access.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

What the app uses

Health & fitness data (Apple Health / HealthKit). During a match, the Padel Streak Apple Watch app reads your heart rate, active energy (calories), and distance so it can show your effort for that match, and it saves the match to Apple Health as a workout. This data is read on your device to display your stats. It is never sent to the developer, used for advertising, or shared with third parties.

Motion data. Your Apple Watch's step counter is used during a match to measure how much ground you cover. This is processed on your Watch and stored with that match. It is never sent to the developer.

Match data you create. Scores, partners, opponents, venues, and any points you tag (for example, who finished a point) are stored so the app can show your history and stats.

Where your data is stored

  • Locally on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Synced through your private iCloud (Apple's CloudKit private database) so your history is restored if you reinstall the app or move to a new device. This uses your own iCloud account; the developer has no access to it and cannot see your data.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not run analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell or share your information with anyone.
  • We do not require or collect an account, email, or login.

Your control

  • You can delete any match in the app.
  • Removing the app removes its local data; you control your iCloud data through your Apple ID / iCloud settings.
  • You can turn health access on or off in the iOS Health app or Settings.

Children's privacy

Padel Streak is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
